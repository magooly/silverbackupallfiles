INSTALLING PORTFOLIO HOLDINGS FROM GOOGLE DRIVE

1. Open the email with the Google Drive link on your Android phone.

2. Tap the link to open the shared file.

3. Tap Download. If you see a preview, tap the three-dot menu, then tap
   Download.

4. When the download finishes, open the Files or My Files app.

5. Open Downloads and tap Portfolio-Holdings-1.4.0.apk.

6. If your phone asks for permission, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this
   source. Go back and tap the APK file again.

7. If you see Install anyway, tap it. Then tap Install.

8. When installation finishes, tap Open.

The app is now installed on your phone. You can find it in your app list.

If you turned on Allow from this source in step 6, you can turn it off again
in Settings under Install unknown apps.
